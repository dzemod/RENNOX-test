Common questions about our renovation services

Timelines, costs, permits and warranty: the things homeowners ask us most.

Still have a question?Call +1 (305) 555-0123
How long does a renovation take?

Smaller projects like a bathroom or bedroom usually take 2–6 weeks. A full home renovation takes 3–6 months, depending on size and complexity. You get a written schedule with your estimate.

How much does a renovation cost?

Every home is different, so we price each project after a free on-site visit. You receive a detailed, fixed-price estimate: no hidden costs and no surprise extras.

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. We visit your home, discuss your ideas and budget, and recommend the best solution, with no obligation to go ahead.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes. All our work is covered by a 5-year workmanship warranty, and materials carry their manufacturer warranties.

Do you handle permits?

Yes. We prepare and file all required building permits, coordinate inspections and make sure every job meets local building codes.

Can I live at home during the renovation?

In most cases, yes. We plan the work in phases, protect your furniture and floors, and clean up at the end of every day.

Can I customize my project?

Absolutely. Every project is tailored to your style, needs and budget, from layout to the last handle.
